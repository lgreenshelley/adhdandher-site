Questions

We've tried everything. How is this different?

Most advice tells you what ADHD is. This hands you what to do: one concrete habit per video, built in the years when there's still time for it to stick. And if it isn't different for your family, the guarantee means you don't pay for it.

My child is only in Year 3. Is it too early?

Not at all. The ADHD brain builds habits slowly and benefits most from long, gradual practice. Starting in Years 3 to 5 gives you the most valuable preparation window. Earlier is better.

Do we need an ADHD diagnosis?

No. These strategies are designed for ADHD girls but work across all neurotypes. On a waitlist, exploring a diagnosis, or simply raising a girl who struggles with the things described? This is for you.
