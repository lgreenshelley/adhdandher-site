Questions

Is this a diagnosis or assessment?

No. It's an educational tool for parents and carers: it maps skills, not conditions, and no diagnosis is needed to use it. Diagnosed, waitlisted, or just wondering about the jump to high school, it's built for you.

What if my child isn't ADHD, or we don't have a diagnosis?

The screener maps skills every child needs for high school, so it works for any child. It's designed with ADHD brains in mind, which makes it especially clear-eyed about the skills that wobble under load, but nothing in it requires a diagnosis.

What ages is it for?

Kids in Year 4 through Year 8. All genders welcome. Already started high school? Years 7 and 8 are squarely in range, and the snapshot shows you where to focus now.

Can I use it more than once?

Yes, that's the design. Retake it each term on the same device and it shows you what has strengthened since last time. One purchase, yours to reuse.

Is my child's information private?

Completely. Your answers are processed on your own device and never sent to or stored by ADHD & Her.

How do I access it?

The screener opens the moment your payment goes through, and the link is in your receipt email. Bookmark it. Open it on any device, whenever suits.
