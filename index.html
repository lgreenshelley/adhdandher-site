About

The OT who was that ADHD kid.

Hi, I'm Shelley. I have been an OT for over a decade, working alongside teachers in primary and high schools, so when I talk about classrooms, I'm talking about rooms I've actually worked in. I'm a mum, so the 7pm homework standoff and the chaotic morning juggle are not theoretical to me. And I grew up with ADHD: the kid who felt too much or not enough, who worked twice as hard to look like she was keeping up.

I don't just understand the research. I've lived it, and I built these courses so families could get the understanding earlier than I did. Hundreds of families have used them to build skills at home.

We're not expecting perfect. Not from your child, and not from you. There will still be lost hats, forgotten homework and big afternoon feelings. That is not failure; that is a nervous system doing its best in a world still catching up.
