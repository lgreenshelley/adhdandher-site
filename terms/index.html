These terms cover everything ADHD & Her offers: this website, our digital products (courses, tools and screeners), occupational therapy consultations delivered by telehealth, and business mentoring for health professionals. By using the website, booking a service or purchasing a product, you agree to these terms. ADHD & Her is operated by Shelley L'Green, AHPRA-registered Occupational Therapist, in Australia.

1. Occupational therapy services

Consultations are delivered by telehealth to parents and carers, and where clinically appropriate the child is the client for record-keeping and health-fund purposes. Services are available to families living in Australia; my AHPRA registration and professional insurance cover Australian practice.

Consultations provide assessment, strategies and support based on the information you share. They are not an emergency or crisis service. If you or your child ever need urgent help, call 000, or contact Lifeline (13 11 14) or Kids Helpline (1800 55 1800). Occupational therapy consultations do not of themselves constitute a diagnostic assessment, and no particular outcome can be guaranteed; recommendations depend on the information available and on strategies being implemented at home and school. Where another kind of support would serve your family better, I will say so and point you in the right direction.

2. Bookings, payment and cancellation

Fees are listed at the time of booking. A deposit is payable when you book an Initial Strategy Session, with the balance charged to your stored card after the session. Screening tools, written summaries, reports and ongoing sessions are charged to your stored card as they are delivered. Card details are stored and processed securely by our payment provider (Stripe, via the Splose practice-management platform); ADHD & Her never sees or stores your card number.

Life with kids is unpredictable, so: you can reschedule or cancel free of charge with at least 24 hours' notice, and your deposit is refunded in full. Cancellation with less than 24 hours' notice, or non-attendance, may incur a late-cancellation fee of up to 50 per cent of the session fee. Repeated late cancellations may require pre-payment of future sessions.

3. Medicare and private health

Rebates depend entirely on your circumstances, your plan and your fund. Medicare rebates generally do not apply to parent-only consultations. Private health rebates for occupational therapy depend on your level of cover; itemised invoices are provided for you to claim. Please check with your fund or Medicare before booking if a rebate matters to your decision.

Mentoring sessions are paid in full at booking and may be rescheduled with at least 24 hours' notice. Testimonials relating to mentoring reflect the individual experiences of the people who gave them.
